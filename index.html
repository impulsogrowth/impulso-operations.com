FAQ

Your Questions.
Answered.

Still unsure? Here's everything you need to know before starting with Impulso.

How Do We Get Started? +
Fill out the intake form — takes 3 minutes. We review your business, your market, and what a good lead looks like for you. Then we schedule a quick call and kick off Week 1 immediately. Content recording starts the first week.
What Do You Need From Us During The Project? +
Access to your Instagram and Facebook accounts, and a few hours in Week 1 for content recording at your job site or business. After that, we handle everything — posting, editing, reporting, ad management. You just close the leads.
Do I Pay For Ad Spend Separately? +
Yes — and that's by design. Your ad budget goes directly from you to Meta. Impulso's fee covers content creation, strategy, and campaign management only. We never touch or mark up your media budget. Most clients start with $10–$30/day.
How Long Before I See Results? +
Organic content starts Week 2. First weekly report in Week 3. Meta ads launch Month 2 with data from the best-performing organic posts. Most clients see their first qualified leads in Month 2 and scale in Months 3–4.
Can You Work With Any Type Of Business? +
Yes. We work with construction, renovation, kitchen and bath, medical, and home service businesses in Miami and South Florida. If you're a service business with a phone number and a need for more customers, we can help.
Is There A Long-Term Contract? +
No. Content-only is billed weekly — cancel anytime. Monthly plans give you a small discount and better continuity for ad campaigns. We earn your business every week. If results aren't there, you're free to walk.
